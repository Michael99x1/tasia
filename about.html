Our Story

Healthcare built around the people who walk through our doors.

Tasia Hospital is a private hospital in Gwagwalada, Abuja offering general and specialist medical care to families, workers, and the wider community. We were established to close a simple gap: reliable, well‑staffed private care that doesn't require travelling across the city or waiting weeks for an appointment.

Our team of resident and visiting doctors, nurses, laboratory scientists, and pharmacists work across nine core departments from routine outpatient visits to surgery, maternity, and emergency care supported by an in‑house laboratory and pharmacy so that diagnosis, treatment, and follow‑up happen without unnecessary delay.

We are proud to serve Gwagwalada and its surrounding areas, and we work with a growing list of HMOs and insurance providers to keep quality care within reach.

Our Mission

To deliver prompt, thorough, and respectful medical care to every patient who walks through our doors regardless of how they arrive.

Our Vision

To be the private hospital Gwagwalada turns to first known for consistency, clean facilities, and doctors who follow through.

Our Standards

What we hold every department to.

01

Clear communication

Every diagnosis and treatment plan is explained in plain language before you leave the consultation room.

02

Clean, monitored facilities

Wards, theatres, and waiting areas are inspected daily against a fixed hygiene and safety checklist.
