Frequently Asked

Common questions before your first visit.

Do I need an appointment for General Outpatient?+

No General Outpatient accepts walk-ins Monday to Saturday, though booking ahead reduces your wait time.

Which HMOs do you accept?+

We work with a range of HMOs, private insurance, and corporate retainerships. Contact our front desk with your provider name to confirm coverage for a specific plan.

Is the emergency unit open at night?+

Yes our Emergency Care department operates 24 hours a day, every day, including public holidays.

Can I get lab results the same day?+

Most routine tests are processed in-house with results available the same day. Specialised tests may take longer, and our team will let you know upfront.

Do you offer ambulance pickup?+

Yes, for genuine emergencies. Call +234 90996258414and describe the situation to dispatch.
