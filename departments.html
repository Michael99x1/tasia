Home/Departments

Nine departments, working as one hospital.

Each department connects directly to our in-house laboratory and pharmacy, so a visit rarely means a second trip elsewhere for results or medication.

General Outpatient

Walk-inMon–Sat, 7am–8pm

Your first stop for everyday illness, routine checkups, and management of ongoing conditions such as hypertension, diabetes, and infections. Consultations are followed by same-visit lab testing and prescriptions where needed.

  • General consultations & physical examinations
  • Chronic disease management (hypertension, diabetes)
  • Vaccinations & routine screening
  • Referral to specialist departments where required

Surgery

By referralElective & emergency

Fully equipped operating theatres for both planned and emergency procedures, backed by a dedicated post-operative recovery ward and pain management support.

  • General and minor surgical procedures
  • Emergency surgery, coordinated with the Emergency Care unit
  • Pre-operative assessment & post-operative recovery
  • Anaesthesia administered by qualified staff

Maternity

24/7 delivery unitAppointment

Antenatal care through to delivery and postnatal follow-up, with a dedicated maternity ward and an on-call obstetric team available around the clock.

  • Antenatal check-ups & ultrasound scans
  • Safe vaginal & caesarean delivery
  • Postnatal care for mother & newborn
  • Family planning counselling

Pediatrics

Walk-inMon–Sat

Child-focused care from infancy through adolescence, delivered in a calmer, child-friendly section of the outpatient wing.

  • Growth & development monitoring
  • Childhood immunisation schedule
  • Treatment of common childhood illness
  • Parent guidance on nutrition & care

Laboratory

Same-day resultsMon–Sat, 7am–7pm

In-house diagnostic testing for patients across every department, reducing the need to travel to an external lab for routine results.

  • Full blood count & blood chemistry
  • Malaria, typhoid & infection screening
  • Urinalysis & pregnancy testing
  • Pre-surgical & antenatal panels

Pharmacy

Open dailyIncl. emergency hours

A fully stocked in-house pharmacy that fills prescriptions issued anywhere in the hospital, with pharmacists on hand to advise on dosage and adherence.

  • Prescription dispensing for all departments
  • Over-the-counter medication & first-aid supplies
  • Medication counselling for chronic conditions
  • Stock checked and replenished daily

Dental

By appointment

Routine and restorative dental care for the whole family, from cleaning and check-ups to extractions and fillings.

  • Routine check-ups & scaling/cleaning
  • Fillings & extractions
  • Treatment of gum disease
  • Oral hygiene guidance for children & adults

Physiotherapy

By referralAppointment

Rehabilitation and pain management for patients recovering from injury, surgery, or living with chronic musculoskeletal conditions.

  • Post-surgical & post-injury rehabilitation
  • Chronic back & joint pain management
  • Mobility & strength therapy programmes
  • Home exercise plans for continued recovery

Emergency Care

A dedicated emergency team on call around the clock, with a resuscitation bay and direct access to surgery, the laboratory, and the pharmacy for fast, coordinated treatment.

  • 24-hour emergency response & triage
  • Resuscitation & stabilisation
  • Direct handover to surgery when needed
